A Holistic Approach to Mission

Evangelism remains central to Shiloh’s mission. Alongside it, the website prominently highlights social service and community development, showing how ministries contribute to the well-being and progress of society.

Our approach

Listen First. Understand Needs. Work Together.

Community initiatives should be based on needs identified with local people, not assumptions. Missionaries are encouraged to listen, understand local circumstances, consider available resources and work towards suitable responses.

Each programme page can explain its purpose, target community, activities, location, partners, evidence, outcomes and next steps.

Mission field activities

Updates from the People and Places We Serve

A living record of pastors’ fellowship, children’s and women’s ministry, church activities, outreach, missionary initiatives and community development work.

Every update should tell the full story

Activity and purpose

What happened, why it was organised and which ministry or community need it relates to.

Date and location

Verified date, country, state, district, town or village. Avoid sensitive details where publication could create risk.

People and partners

Who organised or participated, with names and identifying details only where permission is granted.

Outcome and next step

What was achieved, what participants shared and what further support may be needed.

Regular reporting

A Consistent Way to Collect Field Updates

A simple reporting workflow will help the mission keep its website current and credible. Each missionary or ministry coordinator can submit the activity date, location, ministry area, purpose, description, participants, outcomes, next steps and supporting media.

Suggested activity report fields

Activity details

Title, date, country, state, district, village/town, ministry area and coordinator.

Purpose and description

Need identified, objective, what took place, who was involved and partners.

Impact and follow-up

Verified numbers, outcomes, feedback, challenges and next steps.

Media and permissions

Photo/video files, captions, photographer, consent confirmation and usage restrictions.
