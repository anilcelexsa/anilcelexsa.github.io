Who we are

Sharing Christ’s Love Through Faith, Care and Service

Shiloh Evangelistic Mission brings together evangelistic ministry, missionary engagement and community-focused initiatives. We seek to nurture faith, equip local missionaries and respond thoughtfully to practical needs identified within communities.

Our approach recognises that people and communities have spiritual, social and practical needs. We seek to build relationships, encourage fellowship and support initiatives that strengthen individuals and families.

Our vision & mission

Reaching the Unreached. Equipping People to Serve.

“Whom shall I send? Who will go for us?” — Isaiah 6:8

Our vision
To reach the unreached in India and neighbouring countries through discipleship.

Our mission
To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, empower and send those who are called, plant churches, make disciples, raise leaders, equip fellow missionaries and contribute to the social and economic welfare of communities.
